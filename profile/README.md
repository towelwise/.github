# Towelwise AI

**Know where your towel is.**

Towelwise AI is a suite of plugins and applications designed to keep you all-knowing in your projects. Bring the AI you already use: Towelwise remembers why things were decided, keeps your workspace calm, and teaches you what it all means.

Free and open source · Lives in VS Code · Bring your own AI

## The suite

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Towelwise** | The core VS Code extension: a sidebar to open your AI agents, saved layouts, *Jump to hyperspace*, and a calm beginner profile. |
| **Towelwise Recall** | Memory that remembers the Question: *why* each decision was made. Nothing counts until you approve it. Includes **Total Perspective**, a living constellation of your project's memory. |
| **Towelwise Guide** | A learning hub that opens with *Don't Panic* and teaches with your own code as the examples. |
| **DOUG** | The *Delightfully Optimistic Update Generator*: a cheerful, honest companion app that tells you where every project stands. |

## Our promises

1. You drive your AI. Towelwise never does.
2. Vendor rules first. No gray areas.
3. Nothing counts until you approve it.
4. Memory keeps track of *why*.
5. Free and local where it can be.
6. Calm by default. Nothing steals your keyboard.
7. Cheerful and honest.
8. The tool that grows with you.
